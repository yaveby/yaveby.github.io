02 Approach

Software, like a good space, works best when nothing is in the way.

  1. Clarity

    Start with the real problem. Ask, simplify, and remove what does not need to exist before writing a line.

  2. Flow

    Data and work should move without friction: clean APIs, well-placed messaging, deployments that just happen.

  3. Balance

    Every design is a trade-off between performance, security, cost and time. I make those choices on purpose.

  4. Growth

    Systems and people both evolve. I build things that can change, and I keep learning, most recently with AI.
